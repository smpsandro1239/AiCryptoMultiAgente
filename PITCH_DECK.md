# MATF: The Future of Autonomous Trading

## The Problem
- **Monolithic Bots:** Hard to update, prone to single points of failure.
- **Complexity:** Cryptocurrency markets require multi-faceted analysis (Macro + Micro + Risk).
- **Lack of Traceability:** Why did the bot make that trade?

## The Solution: MATF
- **Multi-Agent Collaboration:** Specialized agents working together.
- **Event-Driven:** Real-time responsiveness.
- **Semantic Memory:** Full audit trail and learning capability.

## Key Features
- **Scalable:** Add unlimited scanners and strategies.
- **Robust:** Built-in risk management and supervisor oversight.
- **Universal:** Integration with 100+ exchanges via CCXT.

## Target Audience
- Systematic Traders
- Crypto Hedge Funds
- Developers building automated wealth management.
